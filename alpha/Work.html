Killer
Network

Gaming Brand Launch Campaign Qualcomm Exit
Problem

A gaming networking brand needed to explain a technical performance advantage in a way gamers would actually care about. Hardware specs alone weren't enough. Lag was the enemy. The brand had to make that personal.

Insight

Gamers don't want networking hardware. They want the lag gone, the game smooth, and the win within reach. The product wasn't a NIC card. It was permission to stop losing to your connection.

Line / Idea
Winning is Killer.
End Lag Now.
System

Brand positioning, campaign strategy, product naming, product messaging, web copy, advertising, packaging design, heat sink design, channel marketing, trade show presence, TV spots, and launch support. Wrote, designed, and shipped end to end. Partnered with Blizzard on a commemorative World of Warcraft coin still collected today.

Outcome
Zero to Qualcomm acquisition in three years.
// Why it matters now

This is product marketing at its cleanest: turn technical differentiation into human motivation. The same challenge lives inside every AI, SaaS, and hardware launch today.

// 02

File­Keepers

B2B Brand & Rebrand Campaign Vertical Marketing
Problem

A records management company serving seven of the top ten law firms in Southern California needed to stand out in a category most people viewed as storage, boxes, and logistics. Operational necessity isn't a brand.

Insight

The buyer wasn't really buying file storage. They were buying space, relief, order, and control. Reclaim the space in your office. Reclaim the space in your head. The product became a solution to a problem no one had named yet.

Line / Idea
Space. The File Frontier.
System

Logo, full rebrand, campaign copy, photography direction, vehicle graphics, sales materials, trade advertising, and vertical-specific marketing targeting law firms, healthcare, and financial services. Work was built and launched end to end.

Outcome
5% year-over-year increase in new clients in targeted verticals. 4% overall increase.
// Why it matters now

This is the same problem many SaaS brands face: the feature may be operational, but the buyer is looking for a more useful future. The line is the bridge.

// 03

Traveler
Guitar

Music & Consumer Brand Platform Registered Trademark 15+ Years
Problem

A portable guitar brand needed a line and brand platform that could hold across products, players, travel contexts, retail, Amazon, trade shows, and advertising over years of growth. Not just a tagline. A brand truth.

Insight

The product isn't just smaller. It gives musicians permission to take the instrument farther — to the airport gate, to the trail, to the place where they're actually living their life. Adventure is the brand, not portability.

Line / Idea
The World's Most Adventurous Guitar.

This line became Traveler Guitar's registered trademark.

System

Brand positioning, product copy, ad taglines, web copy, Amazon Store consulting, six NAMM trade show booths and all supporting materials, print and promotional campaigns, dealer presentations, and speaker decks over fifteen years. Agency689 has been at the helm for a decade of steady year-over-year growth.

Outcome
Line became a registered trademark. Steady year-over-year growth for a decade.
// Why it matters now

A strong product line can become a brand system when it is elastic enough to carry campaigns, commerce, and community. That's the work product marketers need to know how to do.

Sunset
Marquis

Hospitality Agency of Record Voice Governance Since 2004
Problem

A legendary West Hollywood hotel needed to evolve over two decades without losing the rock-and-roll soul that made it famous. The brand had to remain current without chasing trends, polished without becoming sterile, exclusive without feeling corporate.

Insight

The voice had to feel like the hotel already knew you. Understated. Confident. Never trying too hard. The best rock-and-roll doesn't announce itself. The brand voice had to work the same way — earned, not performed.

Line / Idea
Legendary Nights Begin at Sunset.

Long-running voice governance system across hotel, restaurant, spa, villa, email, digital, print, and in-room channels.

System

Agency of Record since 2004. Web copy, email campaigns, seasonal promotions, Cavatina restaurant, The SPA, villa copy, Bar 1200, LIVE@SunsetMarquis concert series, Sunset Marquis Motorcycle Club, print, in-room materials, display ads, brand voice governance, and ongoing creative direction. Adam wrote emails and banners for 15 years and now approves AI-assisted email and banner work.

Outcome
$125K/month in email revenue. 30% ROAS lift when creative returned in-house.
// Why it matters now

This is proof of voice governance over time: keeping a brand recognizable across years, channels, teams, and now AI-assisted workflows. The email program contributes approximately $125,000 per month in attributed revenue. Banner ROAS rose 30 percent after creative direction was brought back from the media company.

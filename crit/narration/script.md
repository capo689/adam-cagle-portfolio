# CRIT — narration script

Voice: direct, discerning, creatively literate. Automated checks should sound rigorous but deliberately incomplete.

## 01 — Brief lock
Campaign review begins with a mid-funnel outdoor brief bound to version four. Any amendment creates a new version; the standard cannot be rewritten after the work arrives.

## 02 — Work intake
A fifteen-second paid-social video, carousel, landing hero, and retargeting email become twenty-two addressable components. OpenRouter routes vision, strategy, voice, comprehension, and summary tasks separately so one model does not review its own interpretation of the campaign.

## 03 — Strategy
A reasoning model compares every asset and funnel transition to the locked audience, proposition, objection, and next action. The work gives trail runners a reason to consider the shoe and carries the promise cleanly from paid social to the landing page.

## 04 — Substantiation
Rules find explicit claims, including a quantified wet-rock grip statement. A multimodal model catches an injury-prevention promise implied by athlete action, terrain, edit, and voice-over. The approved proof library is now a visible step: current product testing clears the grip statement, while the unsupported injury implication is removed.

## 05 — Voice
The legally supportable voice-over still sounds like generic extreme-sports copy. Only the voice-over regenerates, once, and it must pass the original rubric before the review continues.

## 06 — Craft and access
Contrast, delivered type size, captions, safe areas, CTA persistence, and landing continuity are measured in code. A different vision family experiences the campaign small, muted, swiping, and landing. The proposition survives, but the visual idea still feels generic.

## 07 — Rights
Detection surfaces an athlete likeness term and a licensed music cue. A named rights owner—not a model—confirms paid usage, channels, territory, term, and music coverage before the rights gate clears delivery.

## 08 — Direction
Every automated check passes. The Creative Director still returns revise: technically strong, category-familiar, and easy to ignore. The immutable record preserves that reason and sends the missing human dimension back to the evaluation bench.

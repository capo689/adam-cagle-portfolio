# Overview

Adam reported that Agency689 was invited to pitch on the third day after the company emerged from the University of Texas at Austin incubator, competed against three Austin agencies, and won the account. The work continued through the company's later Qualcomm acquisition.

## Adam's Role

Pitch strategy, brand planning, creative and voice direction, product naming, packaging, web, print, television/video, photography, trade-show work, sales and channel materials, and long-term campaign development. Canonical lines include “Winning is Killer,” “Less Lag. More Frag,” and “Kill Faster.”

## Outcome and Qualification

The company was acquired by Qualcomm after approximately three years according to Adam's direct account. Canonical public language is “long-term brand, launch, web, sales, and channel work through its Qualcomm acquisition.” Do not say Adam alone took the company from zero to acquisition or caused the transaction.

## Spoken Explanation

Adam and Agency689 won Killer Network almost at the beginning, named and launched the product and brand, and stayed with the company through its Qualcomm acquisition. The story is about broad founder-stage ownership, not claiming credit for the acquisition itself.

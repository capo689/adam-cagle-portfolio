# Traveler Guitar — Brand Platform and DTC Growth

## Executive Summary

Adam wrote and helped direct Traveler Guitar's long-term brand platform, “The World's Most Adventurous Guitars,” and extended it through ecommerce, product storytelling, campaigns, trade shows, and dealer support. During the broader engagement, DTC revenue grew from approximately $1.1 million to $5 million across Shopify and Amazon.

## Context and Problem

Traveler Guitar needed to make a specialized product category understandable, emotionally appealing, and coherent across customers, dealers, marketplaces, and travel-oriented campaigns.

## Adam's Responsibility

Brand platform, copy, campaign ideas, product messaging, product detail pages, A+ content, email, promotions, print, dealer presentations, and creative direction.

## Strategy

Make travel the organizing idea rather than a feature list. Use a single durable promise to connect product function with identity and adventure.

## Implementation

Shopify and Amazon content, web and product copy, campaign systems, NAMM and dealer materials, promotions, and later AI-assisted creative production.

## Results

Approximate DTC growth from $1.1 million to $5 million. This is a company-level outcome supported by Adam's work; many commercial factors and contributors were involved.

## What Adam Would Change

Not documented. Do not invent an optimization retrospective.

## Lesson

A durable positioning idea can reduce fragmentation across product, channel, and campaign decisions.

## 30-Second Spoken Version

Adam helped build Traveler Guitar around “The World's Most Adventurous Guitars,” then carried that idea into product pages, Amazon content, email, promotions, dealers, and campaigns. The DTC business grew from roughly $1.1 million to $5 million, though Adam is careful not to claim the brand work was the only cause.

# Synthetic Audience Lab — narration script

Voice: evidence-led, skeptical, commercially aware. Never imply that synthetic responses predict market behavior.

## 01 — Evidence intake
The Lab begins with consented, dated, and hashed casino evidence: loyalty tier, visitation cadence, and broad gaming and resort spend bands. Direct identifiers are removed, sensitive behavior is aggregated, and source text remains untrusted data.

Before segmentation, the responsible-gaming gate excludes self-excluded, cooling-off, vulnerability-flagged, and otherwise ineligible guests. It also rejects loss-chasing, credit, urgency, and increased-gambling-intensity objectives. This study may improve an entertainment offer; it may not exploit harm.

## 02 — Panel construction
OpenRouter assigns extraction and persona tasks by measured quality, cost, and provider family. Attributes require source citations. Reproducible behavioral clustering separates social occasionals, slot regulars, table-game weekenders, and resort-entertainment loyalists. Three families construct sixteen profiles from the same eligible evidence.

## 03 — Integrity
Every persona field is known, inferred, or unknown. One persona presents an inference as fact and is blocked before the corrected panel becomes immutable version four.

## 04 — Stimulus
Every profile receives the same midweek room, dining, and entertainment offer and the same fixed question battery. The stimulus never promotes gambling intensity, and an adaptive interviewer cannot steer the panel toward an interesting result.

## 05 — Independent response
Persona authorship is separated from response generation. A balanced family rotation ensures no provider exclusively speaks for the personas it created, and every response runs without seeing the others.

## 06 — Bias control
An alternate family audits persona consistency. The first run converges below the diversity floor, so one bounded rebuild changes only the failed segments. The rebuilt panel must pass the original threshold.

## 07 — Aggregation
Observed variance produces confidence bands around objections and comprehension. Predictive language is blocked; minimum-stay ambiguity becomes a falsifiable hypothesis, not a promise of visitation or gaming lift.

## 08 — Handoff
A human researcher and responsible-gaming owner choose one policy-safe hypothesis worth testing. Its segment, objection, evidence, and synthetic origin enter Conversion Forge, and the eventual real result will grade this panel version.

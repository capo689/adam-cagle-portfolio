# Reading Room — narration script

Voice: calm, useful, and transparent. Abstention should sound like a governed outcome, not a technical failure.

## 01 — Ingest
Every source has an owner, effective date, review interval, and version. Structural chunks preserve procedures and citation paths, while drafts, copies, and superseded documents are excluded before indexing.

## 02 — Index
Dense retrieval carries meaning and lexical retrieval preserves exact identifiers. A read-only MCP server exposes search and fetch operations but contains no write method.

## 03 — Question
The user asks how to reverse a same-day ACH after cutoff. Entitlement filters apply before retrieval, and a small classifier marks the question as multi-source rather than an action request.

## 04 — Retrieval
Hybrid results are reranked with a calibrated cross-encoder. One passage contains instruction-like text and is quarantined by the retrieved-content firewall; the remaining current sources disagree about the cutoff.

## 05 — Grounding
A reasoning model names the conflict but invents a five-PM cutoff. A different provider family rejects that sentence, and the grounding gate fails the whole answer when the supported remainder cannot resolve the request.

## 06 — Authority
The assistant can answer with citations, ask one question, abstain, or hand off. It chooses abstain and gives operations the retrieval trace, scores, conflict, and rejected sentence.

## 07 — Gap capture
The refusal becomes a structured record. Forty-three similar handoffs form a high-cost cluster, turning an apparent assistant failure into a measurable content defect.

## 08 — Growth
A frontier model drafts the missing procedure overnight. A named owner edits and approves it before it may re-enter ingestion. Coverage grows, and reopen rate remains visible beside answered and abstained requests.

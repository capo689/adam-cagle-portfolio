# Overview

- Status: production internal system in daily Agency689 client use.
- Purpose: scale client writing without losing brand context, editorial standards, or human accountability.
- Audience: Agency689's internal creative workflow and client work.
- URL: https://adamcagle.com/wpaper/aiw-whitepaper.html

## Adam's Role

Adam conceived and built the system, wrote the context and persona structures, created governance and review standards, integrated approval routing, used it in daily work, trained or guided users, and retained final editorial judgment. AI assists generation and critique; Adam and the working team remain responsible for selection and approval.

## Architecture and Workflow

Brand-context retrieval, source voice samples, persona specifications, SKILL.md-style governance, multi-draft generation, a standards-review step, Telegram approval routing, human sign-off, and recursive workflow improvement. Exact vendor and database architecture should be discussed only from current system documentation.

## Outcomes

- Used for email, banners, promotional blocks, campaigns, and production copy.
- Reportedly cuts first-draft time by roughly half.
- Demonstrates internal adoption over generic tools because it encodes real client context and editorial rules.

## Limits

Do not publish unconfirmed run counts, draft counts, contributor counts, or client counts appearing on public surfaces. Do not say the system replaces writers or makes final editorial decisions.

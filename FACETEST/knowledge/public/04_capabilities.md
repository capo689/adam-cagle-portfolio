# Capability Map

## AI Systems Design — advanced applied capability

- Definition: converting a business workflow into an LLM-enabled system with tools, context, controls, and an interface.
- Evidence: Singularity SEO, Agent Exchange, VRT2, BookLite, Agency689 Writing Systems.
- Settings: marketing, content, SEO/AEO, market research, agent marketplaces, publishing.
- Tools: Claude and OpenAI APIs, Python, Node.js, MCP/FastMCP, REST, OAuth, PostgreSQL, SQLite, Supabase.
- Credible claim: Adam directly conceives, architects, writes, codes, documents, deploys, and iterates applied-AI systems.
- Do not overclaim: conventional distributed-systems engineering, model training, MLOps, or frontier-model research.

## Workflow Automation — advanced applied capability

- Evidence: n8n, Zapier, API/webhook connections, Telegram approval routing, MCP tools, recurring ingestion and monitoring.
- Strength: mapping the human workflow first, then deciding what automates, augments, recommends, or escalates.
- Limit: no evidence of career-scale Workato administration or enterprise RPA-center ownership.

## Prompt, Context, and Persona Architecture — advanced

- Evidence: sole authorship of prompts, persona files, SKILL.md governance, workflow rules, brand-context systems, and white papers across the Agentic689 suite.
- Credible claim: Adam treats prompts as one layer in a broader context, tool, evaluation, and governance system.
- Limit: do not infer formal model-training or fine-tuning expertise.

## Product Thinking — advanced founder/operator practice

- Evidence: use-case discovery, system taxonomy, prototype-to-production decisions, public beta gating, product safeguards, and iteration across named systems.
- Credible claim: product ownership and zero-to-one product building.
- Limit: not a conventional four-plus-year salaried Product Manager career.

## Copywriting — expert

- Evidence: twenty-five years, Ad Club and Webby recognition, more than sixty accounts, named campaigns, websites, email, product, ecommerce, scripts, presentations, and books.
- Settings: technology, hospitality, gaming, entertainment, finance, ecommerce, professional services.
- Limit: no speechwriting claim.

## Creative Direction — advanced

- Evidence: campaign concepts, launch systems, video and photography direction, naming, brand books, integrated execution, and multidisciplinary team leadership.
- Limit: Adam directs visual work but should not be represented as a traditional hands-on visual designer whose primary craft is design execution.

## Brand Strategy and Voice Systems — expert

- Evidence: brand platforms and long-term voice systems for Traveler Guitar, Sunset Marquis, Hotel Figueroa, Clink Hostels, Killer Network, FileKeepers, NAVIS, and others.
- Differentiator: Adam also encodes voice and standards into AI-assisted systems.

## Client, Delivery, and Agency Operations — expert

- Evidence: co-founding and running Agency689; discovery, pitches, proposals, pricing, staffing, schedules, budgets, vendors, production, and relationships across sixty-plus accounts.
- Limit: do not convert this into formal PMO, PMP, Scrum, procurement-office, or enterprise change-management credentials.

## Leadership and Enablement — advanced practical capability

- Evidence: teams up to fifteen, coaching, documentation, working sessions, practical AI adoption, and movement from user resistance to routine use.
- Limit: no documented enterprise academy, champion network, center of excellence, or organization-wide adoption metrics.

## Research and Synthesis — advanced

- Evidence: naming and brand research, competitive analysis, focus groups, technical research, financial and market-data synthesis, geospatial research, and the Donkey curriculum.
- Limit: not academic research-scientist credentials and not settled expertise in quantum gravity or mining geology.

## UX and Interface Thinking — intermediate to advanced

- Evidence: early interface work, websites, dashboards, Agent Exchange, BookLite, Donkey, Singularity control surfaces, and cross-disciplinary product design.
- Credible claim: Adam designs interaction flows and works where design, language, and engineering meet.
- Limit: do not claim specialist UX-research depth or a portfolio dominated by microcopy.

## Technical Communication — advanced

- Evidence: architecture white papers, implementation guides, governance documentation, system explainers, project documentation, and the Donkey curriculum.
- Strength: translating technical behavior for business and mixed audiences.

## Rapid Prototyping — advanced

- Evidence: multiple working products, betas, open-source systems, and research prototypes built with frontier-model assistance and direct coding.
- Limit: speed does not establish mastery of every technology appearing in a prototype.

## Strategic Planning — advanced

- Evidence: client discovery, business cases, launch plans, competitive programs, project roadmaps, and prioritization across agency and product work.
- Limit: distinguish entrepreneurial strategy from formal corporate strategy-department tenure.

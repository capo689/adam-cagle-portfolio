# Topic Record

Adam maps the current process, identifies the decision or bottleneck, defines what AI does and what a human retains, then connects systems through APIs, webhooks, MCP, n8n, Zapier, authentication, and structured data. He designs validation, monitoring, approvals, documentation, and rollback around the workflow. Evidence spans Singularity, Writing Systems, BookLite, VRT2, and client work. He should not claim enterprise RPA-center leadership or deep Workato expertise.

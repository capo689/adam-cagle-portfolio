# Overview

- Status: public research and technical-communication project; work in progress.
- URL: https://donkey-mauve.vercel.app/
- Curriculum: https://donkey-mauve.vercel.app/curriculum
- Purpose: document an investigation from Physics 101 toward quantum gravity through a thirty-eight-document curriculum and a three-paper evolution.

## Adam's Role

Adam structured the research path, used AI-assisted author/editor/adversarial-review loops, wrote and edited materials, built the static publication, documented uncertainty, and iterated the papers. The project does not establish Adam as a physicist or claim settled scientific results.

## Architecture

Astro, MDX, static-site architecture, technical curriculum, field notes, research documentation, and explicit work-in-progress boundaries.

## Outcomes

Demonstrates research persistence, curriculum architecture, technical writing, source organization, adversarial review, and the ability to explain difficult ideas without hiding uncertainty. No academic peer review or scientific validation is claimed.

## Spoken Explanation

Donkey on the Edge is Adam's attempt to learn a difficult scientific field in public. He built a structured curriculum, used multiple AI roles to challenge the writing, and documented how the ideas changed rather than pretending the project settled quantum gravity.

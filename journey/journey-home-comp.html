What will Sunday be like?

Both morning services are the same and last about 90 minutes, with worship and a message. Stay afterward to meet someone new.

What about my kids?

Journey Kids welcomes children from birth through fifth grade. Start at the kids check-in station; your child receives a name tag and you receive a matching security tag.

Meet Journey Kids ↗
Where do I park?

Come to 61303 S Hwy 97 in Bend. Journey offers golf-cart rides from farther parking spaces to the front door.

Can I talk to someone first?

We’d love to hear from you. Call 541-647-2944 or get in touch with the Journey team.

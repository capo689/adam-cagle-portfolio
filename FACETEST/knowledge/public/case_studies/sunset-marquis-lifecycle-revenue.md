# Sunset Marquis — Long-Term Brand and Guest Lifecycle

## Executive Summary

Agency689 has served as Agency of Record for Sunset Marquis since 2004. Adam's work has extended across the guest lifecycle and multiple brand surfaces, including a program producing approximately $150,000 per month in attributed email revenue and banner work that improved ROAS by 30 percent.

## Context

Sunset Marquis is a distinctive West Hollywood hotel with related dining, music, nightlife, and gallery experiences. Maintaining coherence requires more than acquisition ads; it requires voice and experience across discovery, booking, arrival, stay, events, dining, and return communication.

## Adam's Responsibility

Copywriting, creative direction, email, web, print, out-of-home, in-room material, sub-brand work, client leadership, production, and long-term governance.

## Strategy and Creative Decisions

Preserve a warm, culturally specific hotel voice while adapting it to transactional, promotional, editorial, and on-property contexts. Adam has described his hotel work broadly as guest-lifecycle work.

## Results

- Approximately $150,000 monthly attributed email revenue.
- 30 percent banner ROAS improvement after creative direction returned in-house.
- Client relationship sustained since 2004.

## Evidence Limits

Exact measurement periods, attribution rules, spend, audience size, and baseline campaign details are not public.

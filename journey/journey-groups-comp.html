Meek. Surrender wins.

Sample discussion guide from the material supplied earlier. Matthew 5:5 · not the current week’s confirmed assignment.

1. Meekness begins with surrender

What is the difference between recognizing your need for God and surrendering a decision to him?

2. Meekness is not weakness

Where do you confuse gentleness with weakness? How does Jesus’ example change your view of strength?

3. Take it into the week

Choose one situation where you normally defend yourself or insist on the final word. What might a different response look like?

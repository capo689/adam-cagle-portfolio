# Topic Record

Adam designs AI around explicit roles: generate, retrieve, recommend, critique, wait, escalate, or act within permission. ChatGPT as the Singularity control surface reflects an interest in conversational access to tools. Writing Systems and BookLite preserve human selection; VRT2 presents decision support instead of trading. Adam can discuss practical interface and trust choices, but no formal HCI research credential is claimed.

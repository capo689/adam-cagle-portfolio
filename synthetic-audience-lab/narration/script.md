# Synthetic Audience Lab — narration script

Voice: evidence-led, skeptical, commercially aware. Never imply that synthetic responses predict market behavior.

## 01 — Evidence intake
The Lab begins with consented, dated, and hashed evidence. Direct identifiers are removed, source text remains untrusted data, and behavioral records carry more weight than sales anecdotes. The finance-leader segment clears the evidence floor; a thinner controller segment is excluded rather than invented.

## 02 — Panel construction
OpenRouter assigns extraction and persona tasks by measured quality, cost, and provider family. Attributes require source citations, segments come from reproducible behavioral clustering, and three families construct twelve personas from the same evidence.

## 03 — Integrity
Every persona field is known, inferred, or unknown. One persona presents an inference as fact and is blocked before the corrected panel becomes immutable version four.

## 04 — Stimulus
Every persona receives the same landing concept and the same fixed question battery. An adaptive interviewer is not allowed to steer the panel toward an interesting result.

## 05 — Independent response
Persona authorship is separated from response generation. A balanced family rotation ensures no provider exclusively speaks for the personas it created, and every response runs without seeing the others.

## 06 — Bias control
An alternate family audits persona consistency. The first run converges below the diversity floor, so one bounded rebuild changes only the failed segments. The rebuilt panel must pass the original threshold.

## 07 — Aggregation
Observed variance produces confidence bands around objections and comprehension. Predictive language is blocked; implementation anxiety becomes a falsifiable hypothesis, not a promise of conversion lift.

## 08 — Handoff
A human researcher chooses one hypothesis worth testing. Its segment, objection, evidence, and synthetic origin enter Conversion Forge, and the eventual real result will grade this panel version.

# Topic Record

Adam's strongest craft is copywriting across campaigns, websites, email, ecommerce, scripts, product stories, presentations, naming, and long-form material. He learned through agency leadership and more than sixty accounts. Named work includes Traveler Guitar, Killer Network, Sunset Marquis, FileKeepers, and NAVIS. His current contribution is encoding voice context and standards into systems while retaining human approval. He should not claim speechwriting or a UX-microcopy-specialist portfolio.

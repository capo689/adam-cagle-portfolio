# Topic Record

Adam writes system prompts, persona files, workflow rules, governance documents, and retrieval-supported context systems. He treats prompting as insufficient by itself: useful behavior depends on source context, tool access, structured outputs, evaluation, permissions, and review. Projects include Agency689 Writing Systems, Singularity, BookLite, and Persona Governance System. He can discuss applied RAG, Qdrant, brand-context retrieval, and protected prompts; he should not claim model-training or fine-tuning depth without new evidence.

# Overview

The campaign placed Traveler Guitar products and musicians into travel environments uniquely associated with the brand promise. Client-approved concept ads preceded production.

## Adam's Role

Adam led concept development, client presentation, casting, physical photography, lighting direction, AI environment generation, and final assembly direction. GPT and Grok generated travel environments and individual background elements. Approved concepts established lighting intensity, tone, and gels for photographed talent and products, which were then hand-assembled with generated worlds.

## Creative and Technical Decisions

The process used AI before and after the live shoot rather than attempting to replace the shoot. Concept images aligned stakeholders and production decisions; controlled photography created usable subjects; hand compositing preserved art direction and quality.

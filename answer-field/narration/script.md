# Answer Field — narration script

Voice: analytical and lucid. Make uncertainty and source influence feel useful, not defensive.

## 01 — Query intake
We begin with questions travelers actually asked about a Los Angeles creative weekend, supplemented by clearly labeled synthetic coverage probes. First-party booking behavior stays aggregated, and only public travel language leaves the system.

## 02 — Cluster
Embeddings group similar language, while deterministic labels add trip occasion, neighborhood, amenity, budget band, party type, origin market, and booking stage. AF-044 becomes an audience-intent map rather than a set of invented demographic personas.

## 03 — Freeze set
The normalized intent set is versioned and hashed. A new study may create version nine, but it cannot rewrite the instrument behind version eight.

## 04 — Repeated probes
OpenRouter assigns extraction and judging models by task. The scheduler runs five samples across three engines and three origin markets because one stochastic, location-sensitive answer is not a measurement.

## 05 — Extract
A small structured-output model captures every hotel mention, position, audience phrase, citation, and supporting span. The raw answers remain stored for questions the team has not thought to ask yet.

## 06 — Frame and variance
Two provider families judge whether each mention recommends, lists, or mismatches the hotel. They agree that music-history language is strong but design-weekend visibility is weak. Repeated samples become a sixteen-to-thirty-two percent confidence band with high neighborhood-query volatility.

## 07 — Citation graph
Raw URLs collapse into canonical publishers. The graph reveals the travel publishers and local guides shaping creative-weekend recommendations. Site RAG finds a missing neighborhood-and-occasion evidence asset and weak third-party corroboration.

## 08 — Read-only handoff
The observatory holds no website credentials. One evidence-backed content-and-partnership brief, its audience intent, uncertainty, and source graph move to a separate approval-and-rollback workflow for human review. A batch model then writes the audience intelligence brief, leading with what changed and linking every claim to the frozen study.

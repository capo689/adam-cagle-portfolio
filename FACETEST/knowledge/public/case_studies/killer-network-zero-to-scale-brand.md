# Killer Network — Founder-Stage Brand Through Acquisition

## Executive Summary

Adam reports that Agency689 won Killer Network shortly after its emergence from a University of Texas at Austin incubator. He led a broad program spanning product naming, voice, packaging, web, print, video, photography, trade shows, sales, and channel work through the company's eventual Qualcomm acquisition.

## Problem

A new gaming-network product needed a name, identity, voice, packaging, channel story, and launch system capable of standing out in a performance-obsessed audience.

## Adam's Responsibility

Pitch, strategy, voice, naming, copy, campaign, production direction, web, packaging, sales materials, channel marketing, and long-term creative stewardship.

## Creative Concept

Lines including “Winning is Killer,” “Less Lag. More Frag,” and “Kill Faster” translated performance advantage into the language and competitive energy of gaming culture.

## Results

Agency689 won the account and supported the brand through acquisition. The evidence does not establish that Adam caused the acquisition or quantify his share of product or company growth.

## Lesson

Founder-stage marketing often requires one person to connect positioning, naming, craft, production, sales, channel, and client decision-making rather than operate inside a mature function.

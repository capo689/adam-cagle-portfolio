Agency689
Writing Systems

Lead case study

Problem

Agency work creates constant demand for strong first drafts: emails, banners, promotional copy, campaign concepts, subject lines, landing pages, ad creative, and social copy. The risk with AI is obvious. It can increase volume while flattening voice.

System

Built AI writing workflows for Agency689 client work, including Sunset Marquis, ima Social Club, and Traveler Guitar. Systems use brand context, RAG, recursive learning, SKILL.md files, persona files, and governance rules to create campaign-ready drafts.

Workflow

The system produces multiple first drafts, runs through a second standards AI for quality and voice checks, then routes work for human input through Telegram before anything ships.

Output & Use

Email campaigns, banner copy, promotional copy, landing page copy, campaign concepts, social posts, subject lines, ad creative, and sometimes image and video support. Used daily across Agency689 client work. The Sunset Marquis workflow alone runs roughly three times per week during active campaign cycles.

Outcome
Cuts production time by approximately half. Three or more usable first drafts in less time than one first draft used to take.

This is the practical version of AI-assisted marketing: faster iteration, stronger review loops, and human approval before publication.

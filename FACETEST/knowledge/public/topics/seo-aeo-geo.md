# Topic Record

Adam combines technical SEO, page structure, schema/JSON-LD, internal linking, succinct answer sections, Search Console evidence, rank tracking, competitor monitoring, and frontier-model analysis. His stated premise is that AI answers may occupy the most visible result area, so sites must address answer engines directly in code and content. A reported Sunset Marquis FIFA page produced 30 percent of the prior month's site traffic in direct links during the pre-match period; this story requires stronger canonical documentation before becoming a public metric. Adam can discuss practical SEO/AEO systems, not claim universal ranking guarantees.

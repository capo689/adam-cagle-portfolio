# FACE

An unlinked, non-indexed WebGL experiment: a procedural gold particle membrane on black.

- No source portrait is loaded or rendered.
- The entire viewport is an uninterrupted particle field; the face is a live deformation pushing through it.
- The brow, eye sockets, pupils, nose, cheeks, lips, mouth cavity, jaw, and radiating membrane wrinkles are generated in the vertex shader.
- The face emerges, cocks slightly, silently mouths “hello world,” then straightens and stares.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` holds the portrait in its final pose.

Serve the repository root and open `/FACE/`.

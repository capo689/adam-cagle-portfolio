# Topic Record

Adam has substantial product ownership: discovering needs, defining users and workflows, choosing architectures, setting roadmaps, balancing reliability and speed, building prototypes, deploying, documenting, and iterating. Singularity, Agent Exchange, VRT2, BookLite, and Agency689 Writing Systems provide evidence. He can credibly claim product-builder and founder-operator experience, but not a conventional four-plus-year salaried Product Manager history.

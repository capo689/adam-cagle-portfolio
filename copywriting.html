FileKeepers·Campaign · Brand & Rebrand · Vertical Marketing

Space. The File Frontier.

Campaign

FileKeepers

Records management is not a sexy category. Nobody wakes up excited about bar codes and certified shredding. What they do wake up excited about is the empty room. So we stopped selling the service and started selling what comes after it. Four rooms. Four characters. One log entry. One line.

B2BBrand & RebrandConceptStrategyPrintArt Direction
Problem

A records management company serving seven of the top ten law firms in Southern California needed to stand out in a category most people viewed as storage, boxes, and logistics. Operational necessity isn't a brand.

Insight

Focus groups and customer surveys confirmed it. The number one reason businesses hire a records management company is not compliance, not security, and not cost. It is space. Physical, glorious, reimaginable office space.

Line / Idea
Space. The File Frontier.
Space.Room 1
Space.Room 2
Space.Room 3
Space.Room 4
The Insight · Strategy

Stop selling storage. Start selling possibility.

After focus groups and customer surveys, one answer rose above all others. Businesses do not hire a records management company to store their files. They hire one to get their space back. The brief changed the moment we understood that.

The campaign is a series of empty rooms, each one recently vacated of its files, each one narrated by the person who now owns the possibility. Four rooms. Four characters. One log entry. One line.

Campaign PrintThe File Frontier
System

Logo, full rebrand, campaign copy, photography direction, vehicle graphics, sales materials, trade advertising, and vertical-specific marketing targeting law firms, healthcare, and financial services. Built and launched end to end.

Outcome
5% year-over-year increase in new clients in targeted verticals. 4% overall increase.
Why it matters now

This is the same problem many SaaS brands face: the feature may be operational, but the buyer is looking for a more useful future. The line is the bridge.

NAVIS·B2B SaaS · Hospitality Tech · Campaign & Naming

The phone is mightier than the mouse.

Positioning & Campaign

NAVIS

Bend, Oregon's hometown hospitality powerhouse. Since 1987, NAVIS built the reservation sales and marketing platform that hotels and resorts run on, unifying reservations, revenue, and marketing into a single revenue engine. A full-page B2B campaign that made one argument, again and again, to owners and revenue managers: the call still converts. NAVIS was acquired by Revinate in 2021, forming a market leader serving hundreds of thousands of properties.

PositioningCampaignNamingB2BPrint
Problem

The whole industry was telling hotels to push guests online and trim the call center. NAVIS sold the opposite truth to a skeptical B2B audience: a trained reservations agent converts far better than a website ever will. The campaign had to prove it, not just claim it.

Insight

A website collects traffic. An agent closes guests. NAVIS had the numbers to settle it (46% conversion on the phone against 2% on the web) and the discipline to make technology serve the human instead of replacing them. The pitch was never anti-automation. It was pro-conversion.

Line / Idea
The phone is mightier than the mouse.
Traffic doesn't make reservations, guests do.
Hospitality is not an automated process.
GRM.Naming
The phone is mightier than the mouse.46% vs 2%
The most powerful booking engine.People business
Traffic doesn't make reservations.Guests do
The Idea · Naming

It's not just data. It's what you do with it.

One ad quietly renamed the category. CRM keeps track of contacts, pipeline, and calls. In hospitality that is not enough, so NAVIS reframed it as GRM, Guest Relationship Management, and gave a software pitch a human center of gravity. Every execution carried the same promise the brand was built on. More performance. More profit. Backed by a guaranteed ROI, or your money back.

Full-Page Printthenavisway.com
System

A full-page print campaign with a landing page built per execution (thenavisway.com/GRM, /mightier, /hospitality, /traffic), each one a single headline, a clean argument, the proof, and one call to action. Positioning, headlines, body copy, and the GRM naming, written to make a measurable B2B case to owners and operators.

Outcome
Category leader in hospitality reservation sales. Acquired by Revinate in 2021.
Why it matters now

This is the argument every category is having about AI right now: automate the human, or arm the human. NAVIS picked a side, proved it with data, and built a brand on it. The same case sells AI systems today.

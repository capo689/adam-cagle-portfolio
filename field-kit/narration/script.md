# Field Kit — narration script

Voice: consultative, practical, curious. Emphasize how evidence becomes an executable engagement.

## 01 — Source intake
We start with a consented discovery call and the artifacts the customer actually uses: Slack, screenshots, an SOP, and a spreadsheet tracking compliance handoffs.

## 02 — Normalize
OpenRouter routes speech, vision, and structured extraction tasks to economical specialist models. Every chunk keeps its speaker, timestamp, page, table cell, or screen region.

## 03 — Evidence graph
A retrieval index assigns an evidence ID to every source span. A reasoning model builds the process graph, but every actor, system, wait, and decision must cite that index.

## 04 — Ambiguity
A different provider family audits the graph and finds two assertions the discovery evidence never settled: the CRM write-back owner and the legal-exception path.

## 05 — Scope gate
The customer chooses the safe slice. Evidence preparation and approval summaries are in; CRM writes and legal exceptions are out. The system does not invent engagement scope.

## 06 — Opportunity
Volumes and delays remain ranges where discovery was vague. Deterministic formulas rank the opportunity and produce a two-point-four to four-point-one month payback band.

## 07 — Prototype
A tool-capable model scaffolds one read-only happy path. The sandbox runs it, permits one bounded repair, and verifies that no customer write scope or production credential exists.

## 08 — Engagement packet
The final packet combines the cited graph, evidence index, approved backlog, cost model, runnable prototype, and a shadow-mode pilot plan with owners and kill criteria.

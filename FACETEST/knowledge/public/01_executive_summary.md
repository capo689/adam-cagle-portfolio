# Executive Summary

Adam Cagle's career joins three forms of accountability that are often separated: responsibility for the words, responsibility for client and business outcomes, and responsibility for the working system. He led interactive copy and creative work during the first major agency shift to digital, co-founded and ran Agency689 across more than sixty accounts, and then extended that operating experience into applied generative AI beginning in 2022.

At Agency689, Adam's work spans naming, positioning, campaigns, websites, email, ecommerce, video, packaging, presentations, brand systems, client discovery, staffing, budgets, vendors, and delivery. He has led multidisciplinary teams of up to fifteen and maintained relationships with founders, executives, boards, owners, and clients over long periods. Canonical results include approximately $150,000 per month in attributed revenue from the Sunset Marquis email program, Traveler Guitar DTC growth from roughly $1.1 million to $5 million across Shopify and Amazon, and a 30 percent increase in banner return on ad spend. These outcomes involved broader teams and businesses; Adam's documented responsibility was the associated strategy, copy, creative direction, systems, and delivery work.

Through Agentic689, Adam directly builds AI systems rather than limiting himself to strategy. Singularity SEO is a production product deployed across multiple installations. Agency689 Writing Systems is a production internal system used in daily client work and cuts first-draft time by roughly half. Agent Exchange is a public free beta. VRT2 and BookLite are MIT-licensed open-source systems. AuScan and Donkey on the Edge are research prototypes or research-communication projects. Across them, Adam works with LLM APIs, Python, JavaScript, Node.js, databases, REST APIs, OAuth, MCP, retrieval, automation, deployment, evaluation, and governed human-review patterns.

His strongest value is translation with execution: moving from a messy business need to a clear use case, persuasive language, a functioning prototype or product, documentation, guardrails, and adoption support. He is strongest where AI, content, marketing, product thinking, workflow design, and stakeholder communication overlap. He is less suited to roles centered on conventional software-engineering pedigree, production ML training, MLOps, Kubernetes-scale infrastructure, formal PMO ownership, or specialist data science.

As of August 4, 2026, Adam is pursuing senior remote U.S. work with meaningful ownership, particularly applied-AI building, AI enablement, agentic product work, marketing/content systems, SEO/AEO, technical communication, and hybrid roles that value both writing and implementation.

Summary

Twenty-five years leading creative for product and brand launches. Co-founder, Creative Director, and Lead Copywriter at Agency689, where every launch runs through me end to end: concept, copy, campaign, video, web, email, trade show, packaging, and brand voice systems. Built voice and messaging systems for sixty-plus brands across tech, hospitality, gaming, entertainment, and consumer categories. Sunset Marquis (Agency of Record since 2004), Hotel Figueroa, Killer Network, Traveler Guitar, Xbox, AMD, Microsoft, and FileKeepers among them. Clio, Ad Club, and Webby awards for copy.

Since 2024, sister studio Agentic689 extends the same discipline into AI-assisted creative workflows, SEO/AIO systems, app builds, and web tooling. Production AI writing tools and marketing workflows are in active daily use across Agency689 client work, including a Sunset Marquis writing system used roughly three times per week during active campaign cycles. Built Singularity SEO, an AI-managed WordPress SEO system that connects ChatGPT to site audits, competitive review, page-level SEO/AIO recommendations, Google Search Console visibility, live rank tracking, governed proposals, and rollback-ready change history. Sole author of every system prompt, persona file, skill file, and governance document across the suite.

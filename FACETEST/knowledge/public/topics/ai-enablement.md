# Topic Record

## What Adam Knows
Practical workflow discovery, choosing useful automation points, prompt and agent libraries, context, governance, documentation, human approval, user guidance, and adoption through real work.

## How He Learned It
By introducing generative AI into Agency689 client work beginning in 2022 and building systems used in production rather than operating a formal training department.

## Applied Evidence
Agency689 Writing Systems, Singularity SEO, client guidance, documentation, playbooks, and movement from user resistance to routine use.

## Original Emphasis
Adoption follows usefulness and trust. A team does not adopt AI merely because it attended training; the system must fit its work and preserve decision rights.

## Limits
No formal enterprise academy, champion network, center of excellence, organization-wide adoption metric, or credentialed change-management practice.

# Answer Field — narration script

Voice: analytical and lucid. Make uncertainty and source influence feel useful, not defensive.

## 01 — Query intake
We begin with real comparison queries, supplemented by clearly labeled synthetic long-tail variants. A privacy gate confirms the set is safe for external probing.

## 02 — Cluster
Embeddings group similar language, while deterministic labels add buying stage, entity, and geography. AF-031 becomes a commercial comparison cluster.

## 03 — Freeze set
The normalized query set is versioned and hashed. A new study may create version thirteen, but it cannot rewrite the instrument behind version twelve.

## 04 — Repeated probes
OpenRouter assigns extraction and judging models by task. The scheduler runs five samples across three engines and two geographies because one stochastic answer is not a measurement.

## 05 — Extract
A small structured-output model captures every mention, position, citation, and supporting span. The raw answers remain stored for questions the team has not thought to ask yet.

## 06 — Frame and variance
Two provider families judge whether each mention recommends, lists, or dismisses the brand. Repeated samples become a nineteen-to-thirty-five percent confidence band with high volatility.

## 07 — Citation graph
Seventy-four URLs collapse into twenty-nine canonical publishers. The graph reveals three third-party sources that repeatedly shape comparison answers and a missing evidence asset on the brand site.

## 08 — Read-only handoff
The observatory holds no website credentials. One evidence-backed ticket, its uncertainty, and its source graph move to a separate approval-and-rollback workflow for human review.

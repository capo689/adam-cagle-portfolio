# Creative Skills

## Copywriting

Adam's primary craft credential is lead copywriting. Documented formats include campaign concepts, taglines, headlines, websites, landing pages, email, ecommerce product detail pages, Amazon A+ content, promotional blocks, banner advertising, social copy, video concepts and scripts, presentations, proposals, brand books, long-form content, white papers, product narratives, and technical explanations. His experience spans B2C and B2B work across technology, hospitality, gaming, finance, entertainment, consumer products, and professional services.

## Creative Direction

Adam develops concepts, sets the verbal and strategic direction, briefs and reviews designers and production partners, directs photography and video, and maintains the quality bar through delivery. The Traveler Guitar AI campaign is strong evidence: approved concepts established lighting, tone, and gels for a Hollywood shoot, while GPT- and Grok-generated environments were hand-composited with photographed talent and products. Adam directed casting, photography, lighting, and final assembly. He should not be described as a traditional hands-on visual designer whose primary craft is design execution.

## Brand Strategy, Naming, and Voice

Adam has built positioning, names, taglines, voice systems, tone guides, editorial standards, and long-running campaign platforms. Examples include “The World's Most Adventurous Guitars” for Traveler Guitar; “Winning is Killer,” “Less Lag. More Frag,” and “Kill Faster” for Killer Network; “Space. The File Frontier” for FileKeepers; and “The phone is mightier than the mouse” for NAVIS. Public reuse of individual lines should respect client and portfolio permissions.

## Content Systems

Adam does not treat content as isolated deliverables. Agency work produced reusable templates, standards, content libraries, review processes, and brand systems. Agency689 Writing Systems extends that practice through persistent context, multi-draft generation, standards review, and human approval.

## Storytelling and Technical Translation

Adam translates product behavior and technical architecture into language for customers, executives, mixed technical audiences, and hiring managers. His project white papers and Donkey curriculum show long-form explanation; his brand and campaign work shows compression into memorable language.

## Editing and Editorial Judgment

Documented strengths include copy review, standards enforcement, voice consistency, audience fit, and deciding what AI-generated material is useful. A recurring working principle is that AI can increase volume and variation while human judgment maintains the editorial bar.

## Humor and Creative Writing

Adam is the sole author of the comedy novel *Ship Happens* and reports a Comedy Store background. His listed influences include Carlin, Murphy, Pryor, Kinison, Schimmel, Jeni, and Mel Brooks. Humor is a real part of his voice, but the agent should not reduce his personality to profanity or jokes.

## Presentation and Pitch Craft

Adam led new-business strategy, writing, design direction, and presentations across more than sixty agency accounts. Audiences included founders, executives, owners, boards, marketing leaders, client teams, and creative teams. This supports executive communication and persuasive presentation claims, not keynote-speaking or speechwriting claims.

## Research-Driven Creative Work

Adam directed naming, product, market, competitor, audience, history, geography, flora, fauna, and cultural research. He also facilitated focus groups for consumer electronics, casinos, and hotels. Research was used to shape positioning, names, messaging, and content priorities.

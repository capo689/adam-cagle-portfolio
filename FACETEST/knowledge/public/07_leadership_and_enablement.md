# Leadership and Enablement

## Direct Ownership

- Co-founded and ran Agency689.
- Owned discovery, pitches, proposals, pricing, scope, staffing, schedules, budgets, vendors, production, delivery, and client communication.
- Led and mentored multidisciplinary teams of up to fifteen, including writers, editors, designers, coordinators, producers, developers, photographers, directors, freelancers, and partner agencies.
- Maintained relationships with founders, owners, boards, C-suite leaders, and marketing teams; some client relationships lasted a decade or more.
- Directed copy and creative work alongside a co-founder who led design execution.

## Practical AI Enablement

Adam began integrating generative AI into client workflows in 2022. He has built prompt and agent libraries, standards, documentation, approval flows, and working systems; guided users; and helped move wary teams toward routine use. The strongest documented example is Agency689 Writing Systems, used in daily client work and reported to cut first-draft time by roughly half.

## Process Development

Adam builds templates, content libraries, brand standards, workflow rules, governance documents, technical explainers, operating manuals, and white papers. In AI systems, he defines when a model may act, recommend, wait for approval, or escalate. This is concrete operational work, not abstract advocacy.

## Executive and Cross-Functional Communication

Adam has presented to and worked with owners, founders, executives, boards, marketing leaders, designers, developers, and production specialists. His strongest role is often the bridge between the business objective, the creative idea, and the technical implementation.

## Leadership Philosophy

Supported principles include maintaining a high quality bar, giving specialists room to work, making requirements explicit, and preserving human accountability. Adam responds poorly when preventable errors are repeated after specific feedback. He values collaborators who check their own work before handing it back.

## Difficult Organizational Problems

- Turning ambiguous client needs into a scoped and deliverable program.
- Moving teams from reluctance about generative AI to routine use without removing editorial control.
- Coordinating creative, technical, client, and production contributors across deadlines and budgets.
- Designing automation that improves throughput without hiding failures or exceeding permissions.

## Boundaries

Adam has not documented a formal enterprise training academy, champion network, center of excellence, large learning-and-development function, PMO, or credentialed change-management practice. Appropriate language is practical enablement, coaching, documentation, adoption support, process improvement, and working sessions.

# adamcagle.com

Source for the personal portfolio of Adam R. Cagle: AI systems builder, enablement lead, and writer.

Live site: https://adamcagle.com

## What this is

The static site behind adamcagle.com. It presents twenty-five years of brand and copy work alongside recent production AI systems, organized as two halves of one practice: the systems I build and the words I write.

## Sections

- **AI Systems.** Production AI agents, enablement systems, and SEO/AIO tooling.
- **Copywriting.** Campaign and brand case studies across gaming, hospitality, and consumer work.
- **Managing Director.** Twenty-five years running Agency689: new business, pitches, accounts, teams, and delivery.
- **Technical Writing.** White papers on the shipped systems.
- **UX/UI.** Interface and product work.
- **Creative Writing.** Selected long-form and fiction.

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, deployed on Vercel. No framework runtime. AI-crawler-friendly robots.txt, llms.txt, JSON-LD, and per-page metadata.
